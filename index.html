THE MARKET CHALLENGE01 / 09

Global uncertainty is changing
how companies think about growth.

Resilience now means looking beyond familiar markets, suppliers and business relationships.

01

Geopolitical uncertainty

Trade tensions, shifting regulations and supply-chain disruption make over-reliance on a single market or partner increasingly risky. Companies need to diversify where they sell, source and build relationships.

↗
02

Promising markets are not always easy to read

Market size alone does not show where demand sits, how purchasing decisions are made or which route to market is commercially realistic.

↗
03

The right partner is not easy to identify

A long list of potential distributors, buyers or suppliers is not enough. Companies need partners with the right capability, reach, credibility and commercial fit.

↗
04

Distance can slow good opportunities

Without someone close to the market, introductions, meetings and promising discussions can lose momentum before they become real business.

↗

Indonesia can be part of the answer — with the right insight, partners and local follow-through.
